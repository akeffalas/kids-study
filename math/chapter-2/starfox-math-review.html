Answer Key

For the General grading the mission
  1. Missing factors: Lesson 2-1, p. 66
    • a. 7 × 8 = 56    b. 7 × 9 = 63    c. 25 × 1 = 25 (c is the Identity Property)
  2. Name the property: Lesson 2-2, p. 68
    • a. Commutative (factors swapped order)    b. Zero (anything times 0 is 0)
    • c. Distributive (the 6 multiplies both parts of 40 + 3; both sides equal 258)
    • d. Associative (same order, regrouped parentheses; both sides equal 900)    e. Identity (times 1 stays the same)
  3. Pattern: 560, 5,600, 56,000, then 50 × 400 = 20,000 Lessons 2-3 and 2-4, pp. 70–73
    Multiply the basic fact, then attach every zero from both factors. Part d is the trap: 5 × 4 = 20 already ends in a zero, and the three zeros from 50 and 400 still go after it. Watch for 2,000.
  4. Estimating: Lesson 2-5, p. 74
    • a. 500 × 60 = 30,000 gallons (487 rounds to 500, 62 rounds to 60)
    • b. 3,019 is far too small. The estimate is about 30,000, so the answer should have five digits. (The exact answer is 30,194. Slippy probably forgot the placeholder zero when multiplying by the 6 tens of the multiplier.)
  5. Zeros in the multiplicand: 3,075 × 4 = 12,300    4,008 × 6 = 24,048 Lesson 2-6, p. 76
    The multiplicand is the number being multiplied (the top number). Watch for 2,448 in part b: that means a zero was skipped instead of held as a placeholder.
  6. 348 × 27 = 9,396 miles Lesson 2-7, p. 78
    348 × 7 = 2,436   348 × 20 = 6,960   2,436 + 6,960 = 9,396
  7. 526 × 314 = 165,164 bolts Lesson 2-8, p. 80
    526 × 4 = 2,104   526 × 10 = 5,260   526 × 300 = 157,800   total 165,164
  8. 473 × 208 = 98,384 feet Lesson 2-9, p. 82
    473 × 8 = 3,784   473 × 200 = 94,600   3,784 + 94,600 = 98,384
    The multiplier (208) has a 0 in the tens place. That row is all zeros and can be skipped, but the next row must still shift two places. Watch for 13,244, which comes from shifting only one place.
  9. $12.45 × 36 = $448.20 Lesson 2-10, p. 84
    Multiply as if it were 1245 × 36 = 44,820, then put back the two decimal places. Estimate check: $12 × 36 is about $430.
  10. Final Mission: Fox's change is $36.00 Lesson 2-11, p. 86
    • 1. 18 × $4.25 = $76.50 for Smart Bombs
    • 2. 35 × $2.50 = $87.50 for Shield Boosters
    • 3. $76.50 + $87.50 = $164.00 spent
    • 4. $200.00 − $164.00 = $36.00 change
    The hidden step is the total cost: the problem asks for change, so the student has to find what was spent first.
