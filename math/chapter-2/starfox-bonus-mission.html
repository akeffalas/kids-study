Answer Key

BONUS
For the General grading the mission
  1. Multipliers ending in zeros: Lessons 2-3 and 2-9, pp. 70, 82
    • a. 312 × 8 = 2,496, zeros: 1, so 312 × 80 = 24,960
    • b. 423 × 6 = 2,538, zeros: 3, so 4,230 × 600 = 2,538,000
    In b, one zero comes from 4,230 and two from 600. Watch for 253,800, which counts only the zeros in 600.
  2. Choose the method: p. 75; Chapter Test #30–31
    • a. M   b. P   c. M   d. P
    • e. a = 15,000    c = 54,000 (either order)
    • f. Accept any basic fact with zeros attached, solved correctly (for example, 70 × 800 = 56,000).
    Rule of thumb: basic fact plus zeros is mental math; anything that needs regrouping or partial products is paper and pencil. For reference, b = 17,316 and d = 98,544.
  3. Cents only: Lesson 2-10, p. 84
    • a. $0.35 × 48 = $16.80
    • b. Falco put the decimal point in the wrong place: he counted one decimal place instead of two. Accept any answer that names the decimal point.
    35 × 48 = 1,680, then write the decimal point two places from the right. Estimate check: about 50 × $0.35 = $17.50, so $168 is far too much.
  4. Test-level challenge: Lessons 2-5 and 2-8, pp. 74, 80
    • a. 6,000 × 400 = 2,400,000 (6,284 rounds to 6,000; 357 rounds to 400)
    • b. 6,284 × 357 = 2,243,388 miles    c. YES
    6,284 × 7 = 43,988   6,284 × 50 = 314,200   6,284 × 300 = 1,885,200   total 2,243,388
    Watch for 546,708: that comes from shifting the hundreds row (6,284 × 3) only one place instead of two. If your child circles YES next to an answer like that, go over what "close" means.
  5. Distributive Property: Lesson 2-2, p. 68; Chapter Test #29
    • a. 6 × (300,000 + 40) = 1,800,000 + 240 = 1,800,240 miles
    • b. A full answer says: break 300,040 into 300,000 + 40, multiply each part by 6, then add the two products. Accept any explanation with those three steps.
